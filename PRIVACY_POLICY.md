# Privacy Policy for Cyrillic Passwords

**Last updated:** August 6, 2026

**Nootek Co. Ltd** ("we", "our", or "us") operates the **Cyrillic passwords** mobile application (package ID `org.passwdhelper.app`).

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use

**Cyrillic passwords does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or sensitive information.**

- **Offline Functionality:** The application functions 100% locally on your device.
- **No Network Requests:** The app does not request or use internet access permissions (`android.permission.INTERNET`). No data is sent to external servers or third parties.
- **No User Accounts:** You do not need to register, log in, or provide personal details to use the application.

---

### 2. Permissions

The application does not request any sensitive permissions (such as location, camera, contacts, microphone, or external storage).

---

### 3. Clipboard Usage

When you use the "Copy Password" feature, the app converts the text locally on your device and copies it to your device's system clipboard. On supported operating systems (Android 13+), clipboard data is marked as sensitive (`EXTRA_IS_SENSITIVE`) to ensure local security.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services

The application does not use any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, analytics tracking services, or external APIs.

---

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
